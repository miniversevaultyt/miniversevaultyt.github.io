What is MiniVerse Vault?

MiniVerse Vault is more than a collection channel. It is a place for miniature worlds, childhood memories and the stories behind the things we love.


From a 1:64 diecast sitting on a shelf to a detailed LEGO spacecraft assembled piece by piece, every collectible has something to say. Some remind us of a movie. Some take us back to childhood. Others represent a racing legend, a favourite character or simply the joy of building something with our own hands.

How the Vault Began

I initially started collecting toys being an adult kid when I was around 34 and slowly it just grew into a hobby which spills into my room which is full of LEGOs, diecasts and collectibles.

But what's different? Well nothing, not everything is to be different—be what we all are.

Another reason LEGOs really makes me get on my toes is that it helps me stay focused and also get into a completely parallel universe where I learn some real management lessons.

The Person Behind the Vault

Naitik Gajjar

MiniVerse Vault was founded by Naitik Gajjar, driven by a simple fascination with miniature worlds and the memories attached to them.

What began at age 34 as an interest in LEGO and miniature vehicles evolved into a broader passion for collecting, building, photographing and sharing pieces of the worlds that inspire focus and mental clarity.
