What is MiniVerse Vault?

MiniVerse Vault is more than a collection channel. It is a place for miniature worlds, childhood memories and the stories behind the things we love.


From a 1:64 diecast sitting on a shelf to a detailed LEGO spacecraft assembled piece by piece, every collectible has something to say. Some remind us of a movie. Some take us back to childhood. Others represent a racing legend, a favourite character or simply the joy of building something with our own hands.

How the Vault Began

It started with a feeling many of us know.

As children, we saw miniature cars, LEGO sets, action figures and movie characters on shelves. Some of us owned them. Some of us could only look at them and imagine the worlds they belonged to.

Years later, that fascination never really disappeared. It simply grew up.

MiniVerse Vault was created from that idea: that growing older doesn't mean leaving those memories behind. Sometimes it means finally having the opportunity to bring them home.

What began as an appreciation for miniature cars and LEGO gradually became something much bigger — a universe containing racing legends, movie vehicles, characters, spaceships, aircraft, Formula 1 machines and builds that deserve a place on the shelf.

The Person Behind the Vault

Naitik Gajjar

MiniVerse Vault was founded by Naitik Gajjar, driven by a simple fascination with miniature worlds and the memories attached to them.

What began as an interest in LEGO and miniature vehicles evolved into a broader passion for collecting, building, photographing and sharing pieces of the worlds that inspire us.

The idea behind the channel is not simply to show what is in the collection, but to share the feeling behind it — the excitement of opening a new box, the patience of a long LEGO build, the detail of a 1:64 model and the nostalgia of seeing a favourite character or vehicle brought to life in miniature.

"Some memories are too big to forget.
Sometimes, we keep them small enough to fit on a shelf."
